# INTRODUCTION TO VISTAAR

**VISTAAR – Virtually Integrated System to Access Agricultural Resources**

VISTAAR is an open-source, interoperable, and federated public network poised to revolutionize the agriculture sector.

#### **About VISTAAR**

VISTAAR (Virtually Integrated System to Access Agricultural Resources) is an open-source, interoperable, and federated public network designed to transform the agriculture sector. Its mission is to democratize and contextualize knowledge, empower farmers' voices, and amplify the collective wisdom of the ecosystem through exponential AI.

This dynamic network will play a pivotal role in enabling the discovery and fulfillment of verified agricultural content, best practices, and agri-skilling across diverse public and private provider platforms. Ultimately, it aims to drive innovation and progress in agriculture through a collaborative approach.

VISTAAR creates a two-way channel for engagement, participation, and representation within the agricultural ecosystem. It contributes to the Government of India's vision of doubling farmers' income by actively involving farmers in discussions and decision-making processes.

By understanding farmers’ needs, successes, and challenges, VISTAAR empowers them to share innovations and access relevant information, enabling informed decision-making for their farms.

A key strength of the VISTAAR model is its ability to enable a federated network of ecosystem contributors and collaborators across domains such as:

* Information and advisory services
* Goods and services marketplaces
* Credit services

This approach ensures improved quality and brings innovative practices directly to farmers and agricultural extension workers.

Additionally, VISTAAR’s network governance framework—driven collectively by the Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare (MoAFW) and state governments—ensures seamless integration with India’s population-scale Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) initiatives (such as UPI), while safeguarding farmers' interests.

VISTAAR’s core architecture is designed to protect beneficiary data, standardize content quality, and enable future-ready innovation, integrity, and accessibility.

The platform will also be supported by an AI services layer that simplifies onboarding for both content providers and users. This layer will include a suite of evolving services that enable decoupled information flow across the network using standardized protocols.

The AI layer will leverage natural language processing (NLP) to interpret content and user queries, enhance response quality, identify user intent, and route queries to the most relevant sources across the network.
