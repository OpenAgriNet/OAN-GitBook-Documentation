# Roles You Can Play

**Provider Platform:** If you create or facilitate agriculture-related verified content, best practices, scheme information, videos, farming techniques, etc., and aspire to broaden your reach to farmers, governments, and field-level workers via a web page, mobile application, or web application or plan to build one in the future you can join VISTAAR as a Provider Platform.

**Provider:** If you would like to be onboarded onto an existing provider platform as a contributor of agri-related content, you can join the network as a Provider.

**Seeker Platform:** If you are an application or platform provider that acts as an interface for farmers, students, and field-level workers to access agricultural information and advisory resources from various sources or aspire to become such a platform you can join VISTAAR as a Seeker Platform.

**Seeker:** If you would like to consume the information made available by the VISTAAR network through any of the seeker platforms, you can join as a Seeker.

**Network Service Provider:** If you aim to enable other organizations to join this network through your technology, business, or onboarding services, this role is best suited for you.

You have the flexibility to choose one of the above roles or even assume multiple roles within the network, enabling you to actively engage in and benefit from the diverse opportunities provided by VISTAAR.
